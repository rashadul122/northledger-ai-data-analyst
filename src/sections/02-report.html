The report

RentSafeTO Building Health, as an interactive report

A Power BI-style report built in the browser from City of Toronto open data, with evaluations to {{f rentsafe_meta:meta.data_as_of}}. Use the slicers: the cards, the Portfolio pulse and Pillars pages and the scorecard below filter together; Points per fix and the risk chart follow the district and ward only, and the trend chart follows a single ward. Tap or click a ward bar or map area to filter to it; tap another to switch. Clear a filter with the × on its chip or with Reset filters. With a keyboard, Ctrl or Cmd adds more wards and Esc clears the last filter. Public view is ward and district level only: no landlord, firm or address is ranked, and a view of fewer than five buildings shows no values.

Independent analysis by NorthLedger Insights of the City's open data. It is not produced by, affiliated with or endorsed by the City of Toronto or RentSafeTO.

Measures

Each measure the report runs, in plain words, with the formula the browser actually computes. These are written for this RentSafeTO data; none of them comes from the owner's separate PL-300 project.

Buildings
Count of buildings in view, each at its latest City evaluation. sum(n)
Units covered
Confirmed units in those buildings. sum(units)
Average City score
Mean of the City's current building score over the buildings in view; each building counts once. sum(score_sum) / sum(n)
Average City score, unit-weighted
The same mean, weighted by units, so it reads as the score the average tenant lives with. sum(score_usum) / sum(units)
Green, yellow and red share
Share of buildings whose City score falls in each lobby-sign band. sum(green) / sum(n)
Evaluations with a refusal, per hundred
Evaluations with at least one area scored zero (refused or blocked), per hundred evaluations. sum(refused_evals) / sum(n) * 100
Pillar score
For one building: the weighted share of full marks over the pillar's items, using the City's published tier weights, with not-applicable items left out and, on the default basis, refused or blocked items (scored 0) also left out and counted separately as refusals; the City basis counts them as zero points. For a view: the mean over buildings. sum(p_sum) / sum(p_n); City basis sum(p_csum) / sum(p_cn)
Proactive score (change and trend)
The hero's "What changed", the trend chart, Analysis B and the control chart use the City's proactive building score, not the current score the cards use: reactive deductions are recorded only on each building's latest evaluation, so only the proactive score compares one evaluation with the next.
Points per fix
City points gained by raising one item to full marks, averaged over every building, from the City's own formula. Read from Analysis A's ward table; it follows the district and ward filters, not the year or property-type filters.
District and City rows
The simple average of the member ward cells, each ward counted once, like a regional row in a league table.
