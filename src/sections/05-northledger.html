NorthLedger, the engine

One recorded run: the City's files through the engine

NorthLedger is the audit engine behind the Data Health Audit. It lands a file, scans it for personal data, asks a person to decide what to keep, scores its health, cleans it with every rejected row quarantined and counted, records each finding as a re-runnable fact, gates it (RECOMMEND, WATCH or INSUFFICIENT), writes the brief and exports a Power BI project. How we caught our own mistakes

  • The cleaner threw away almost everything. The first end-to-end test of a deliberately messy file kept {{f engine_scorecard:fixture.before.clean|int}} of {{f engine_scorecard:fixture.rows|int}} rows ({{d fixture_before_quarantine_pct}} quarantined) and still gated that finding RECOMMEND. A date rule fired on a single date-like value. Fixed with a regression test proven red first: now {{f engine_scorecard:fixture.after.clean|int}} clean and {{f engine_scorecard:fixture.after.quarantined|int}} quarantined ({{d fixture_quarantine_pct}}).
  • An AI analyst's total did not add up. A replayed session stated {{d s5_stated}}; the parts it had queried add to {{d s5_correct}} ({{d s5_parts}}). The replay page now carries the correction, and the engine's findings carry no hand-passed values.
  • The first forecast on this page lost to a naive guess. Its {{f forecast_lab:display.v1_in_sample_mape}} error was measured on its own fitting months; out of sample it missed by {{f forecast_lab:display.v1_holdout_recent_mape}} against {{f forecast_lab:display.v1_holdout_recent_naive_mape}} for repeating the last value. The Forecast Lab replaces it.
  • An earlier traceability claim was withdrawn. The replay audit now reports what it measured: {{d replay_traced}} of {{d replay_stated}} stated numbers ({{d replay_trace_pct}}) matched a query result exactly, and {{d replay_q_reproduced}} of {{d replay_q_checked}} queries reproduced.
