Four deep-dives, each with its limits

The kind of monthly deep-dive the retainer includes. Each card runs Question, Method, Chart, Finding, Limits and how to reproduce it. All of it is numpy and pandas code in this repository, written with AI assistance; it uses no model library and calls no AI model when it runs.

Finding
The City's published weights reproduce {{d a_exact}} scores exactly ({{d a_zero_all_share}}). On rows with a refused item, counting it as zero reproduces {{d a_zero_rows_share}}; leaving it out reproduces {{d a_missing_rows_share}}, so the City counts a refused area as zero points. A free least-squares fit tested on the latest evaluations scores R squared {{d a_ols_test_r2}} with a mean error of {{d a_ols_test_mae}} points, against {{d a_formula_test_mae}} for the published formula, and gives a high-to-cosmetic ratio of {{d a_ratio_recovered}} against the published {{d a_ratio_published}}. The same fit on scores produced by the City's exact formula also gives {{f rentsafe_analysis_a_points_per_fix:unconstrained.ratio_on_formula_scores|fixed:2}}, so the gap is the straight-line fit's approximation, not a sign that the published weights are wrong. {{d a_unexplained}} evaluations stay unexplained.

Maintenance archetypes and a control chart

Question
{{f rentsafe_analysis_d_archetypes:archetypes.question}} {{f rentsafe_analysis_d_archetypes:control_chart.question}}
Method
{{f rentsafe_analysis_d_archetypes:archetypes.method}} {{f rentsafe_analysis_d_archetypes:archetypes.shape_variant.method}} {{f rentsafe_analysis_d_archetypes:control_chart.method}}
Finding
No distinct archetypes. On raw levels the silhouette picks {{d d_k_level}} clusters, which only separates buildings that are good everywhere from buildings that are weak everywhere. The shape variant finds a group of {{d d_lag_n}} buildings whose {{d d_lag_pillar}} pillar sits {{d d_lag_gap}} points below their own average; {{d d_lag_green}} of them hold a green sign (silhouette {{d d_lag_silhouette}}, against {{f rentsafe_analysis_d_archetypes:archetypes.shape_variant.null_silhouette.shape|fixed:2}} for the same data with each pillar shuffled). A one-line rule, {{f rentsafe_analysis_d_archetypes:archetypes.shape_variant.one_line_rule.rule}}, agrees with that group on {{d d_rule_agreement}} of buildings, so it is a single weak pillar, not a type of building. The control chart flags {{d d_flags}} of {{d d_months}} eligible months (months with enough evaluations to judge). Survival analysis (time from green to first yellow) was not run for this version.
